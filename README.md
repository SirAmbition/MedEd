# Medical Education Instagram: Project README

> Working document. Update as decisions are made. Last updated: 14 Nov 2026 (v0.6)

---

## 1. Overview

A faceless medical education Instagram account posting visual, high-yield content (infographics and carousels) for medical students and junior doctors. It starts as a free information page to build an audience and trust, then sells digital study products.

**Goal:** A$1,000/month in mostly passive income within 12 to 18 months.

**North Star (production):** Upload one guideline PDF, and Claude produces everything needed to post: post plan, fact-checked slide text, finished slides or ChatGPT image prompts, captions, alt text, Pinterest copy and a posting checklist. My only jobs are two short checkpoints (approve the plan, final review) and posting. See section 5.

**Constraints (non-negotiable):**
- No face, no video of me, no voice required
- No tutoring, coaching or 1:1 client work
- No working for other people
- Low to no upfront cost
- Mostly passive once set up: maintenance over constant input

## 4. Content plan

### Weekly series (4 posts/week)

Named series train followers to expect content and make batching predictable.

| Day | Series | Format | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mon | **Approach** | Carousel, 7 to 10 slides | "Hyperkalaemia: step-by-step management" |
| Wed | **Quick Ref** | Single infographic | "ECG changes in hyperkalaemia" |
| Fri | **Friday Case** | Question carousel (QBank preview) | Vignette, answer on last slide |
| Sun | **Side by Side** | Comparison carousel | "SIADH vs CSW vs DI" |

**Posting time:** 5:30pm AWST (8:30pm AEDT in summer), when east coast students and doctors are home. Adjust once Instagram shows when followers are online.

### Content calendar

Live calendar (topics, slide outlines, CTAs, sources, status): Instagram Content Calendar sheet (link: _add here_)

### Rollout: how content builds gradually

**Launch day (Mon 30 Nov 2026): 3 posts, all pinned**
A new visitor judges the page by its first 3 posts, so launch with a full first row instead of one lonely post:
1. Welcome carousel: what the page is, who it's for, the weekly series
2. Flagship: ABG interpretation in 8 slides
3. Flagship: Called to a deteriorating patient, A to E

**Week 1 (acid-base): foundation**
- Easy, high-save formats only: Approach, Quick Ref, Side by Side
- No Friday Case yet: question posts need followers to comment, so they flop on a brand new account
- Single-image Quick Refs are the fastest format, used to bed in the workflow
- CTA points to lead magnet #1 (ABG cheat sheet)

**Week 2 (electrolytes): add interaction**
- Launch the Friday Case series
- Check week 1 analytics before making week 2: double down on the format with the most saves

**Week 3 (intern prep): ride the January wave**
- New 2027 interns start in January and search for prep content in December
- Themes: AKI, fluids, delirium, on-call basics
- Tease lead magnet #2 (Intern on-call cheat sheet) for January

**After week 3**
- January: "Intern survival" month, launch lead magnet #2
- Level: pitched at final-year to BPT level from the first post. Topics can be common, but the depth must not be basic (see section 2, Content level)
- Every week works as a mini-series on one theme, which later becomes a notes product

### Rules that keep the schedule realistic
- **Two-week buffer:** always have 8 posts finished and scheduled before the current week. If the buffer drops below 4, pause new topics and only repurpose.
- **Batch, don't drip:** one session every 2 weeks makes 8 posts.
- **Effort ladder:** Quick Ref (about 30 min) < Side by Side (about 45 min) < Friday Case (about 45 min) < Approach (about 60 to 90 min).
- **Heavy rotations or exam blocks:** drop to 3 posts/week (skip Side by Side) rather than stop completely.
- **Lead magnet before CTA:** never post "link in bio" before the thing in the link exists.
- **Add trending audio to carousels:** pushes them into the Reels feed for extra reach.

### Content pillars
1. Core concepts (physiology, pathophysiology)
2. Clinical approach (how to work up X)
3. Ward and on-call survival
4. Exam-style questions
5. Pharmacology quick references

### Carousel structure (template)
1. **Hook slide:** clear title, the problem it solves
2. **Slides 2 to 8:** one idea per slide, minimal text, visuals
3. **Summary slide:** key takeaways
4. **CTA slide:** "Save this for your next shift" / "Free cheat sheet, link in bio"

### Caption template
```
[One-line hook]

[2 to 4 lines of context or key point]

Save this for later and share with a colleague.
Free [lead magnet name], link in bio.

Educational content only, not medical advice.
References: [source]

#hashtags (3 to 5 relevant ones)
```

### Batching workflow
- One session every 2 weeks: create 8 posts (first batch before launch: 15 posts)
- Schedule with Meta Business Suite (free)
- Cross-post to Pinterest the same day

## 5. Production workflow: one PDF in, ready-to-post package out

### The goal
I upload a guideline PDF and say "run the pipeline". Claude's agent team (section 6) does everything up to the point where I save the images and post. I stay in the loop at two checkpoints because I am the registered practitioner whose name is on the accuracy.

### How a run works
```
Upload PDF or notes (default: 4 posts, one per series)
 1. Supervisor: brief; reuse cached extract if this source was seen before
 2. A1 Planner-Writer: plan, self-scored on the series rubric
 3. Supervisor: plan approval (auto if every rubric line is 4+)     <- Supervisor's content review happens here
 4. A1 Planner-Writer: slides.md in the canonical format
 5. GM A: accuracy check (fixes small things, max 2 loops for big ones)
 6. Script: check_text.py (word limits, dashes, US spellings)
 7. A2 Compliance: paraphrase closeness and AHPRA only (max 1 re-check)
 8. Supervisor: freeze review. Last chance to change text. Then slides.md is FROZEN
 9. Script: build_package.py (prompts, alt-text scaffold, caption skeleton, checklist)
10. B1 Visual and Packaging: polish (layouts, alt-text visuals, caption, Pinterest)
11. Script: check_sync.py (one final consistency check)
12. Supervisor: report to ME. Later: B3 Image QA on my images; B2 renders any slide that fails twice
```
Steps run in order on purpose: v0.5 ran some in parallel and reviewers ended up checking stale drafts. Parallelism now happens across posts (4 posts move through the line together), not within one post.

### Two ways to make the images
| | Mode A: Claude renders slides (recommended) | Mode B: ChatGPT renders slides |
|---|---|---|
| Who puts text on the slide | Slide Assembler, word for word from the approved text | ChatGPT, from a prompt containing the text |
| Accuracy risk | Very low: text is exactly what was fact-checked | Higher: AI images still misspell words, change numbers and mislabel anatomy |
| ChatGPT's role | Optional illustrations (no text), saved into the post folder | Whole slide |
| Extra step | None | Upload images back for Image QA |
| Best for | Quick Ref tables, Side by Side, flowcharts, anything with numbers | Illustration-heavy hook slides |

Rule: any slide containing a number, dose, threshold or label uses Mode A, or passes Image QA before posting. Never post an AI image of a real-looking patient.

### Output: one folder per run
```
runs/2026-11-20_hyperkalaemia/
  00_source.md            citation, issuing body, version, date, licence note
  01_extract.md           key recommendations with page numbers
  02_plan.md              approved post plan
  calendar-rows.csv       rows to paste into the content calendar
  run-summary.md          what was made, what failed, what needs me
  post-01_approach/
    slides.md             approved slide text
    accuracy.md           GM A accuracy review
    compliance.md         PASS / FIX log
    image-prompts.md      one ChatGPT prompt per slide
    final/slide-01.png    finished slides (Mode A), 1080x1350
    caption.txt           caption, hashtags, references
    alt-text.txt          accessibility text per slide
    pinterest.txt         pin title and description
    checklist.md          every check ticked before it can be posted
```

### What I still do (about 10 to 15 minutes per post)
- Checkpoint 1: approve the plan
- Checkpoint 2: read the final slides against the guideline (doses and thresholds always manually)
- Generate any ChatGPT images and save them
- Schedule in Meta Business Suite and cross-post to Pinterest

### Choosing guidelines
- Prefer openly published guidelines (e.g. ACSQHC standards, state health guidelines, KDIGO, society guidelines)
- Check each guideline's licence: facts can be taught, but wording, tables and figures can't be copied
- Don't upload subscription content (e.g. eTG, AMH) if its terms forbid it; use it only to check my own work
- Use the current version: the Ingestor flags anything superseded or more than 5 years old

### Build order (how we get there)
1. **Lock the brand kit** (colours, fonts, logo). Blocks the templates.
2. **Weeks 1 to 3: run the agents manually**, one at a time, on the launch calendar. Tune each prompt.
3. **Save each agent as a Claude skill** once its output is reliable.
4. **Build the Slide Assembler templates**, one per series (Approach, Quick Ref, Friday Case, Side by Side).
5. **Build the Project Supervisor and the two General Manager skills** ("guideline-to-posts") that run the teams with the two checkpoints.
6. **Test on 3 guidelines**, compare against manual posts, fix gaps, then make it the default.

### Prompt library
All agent prompts live in section 6. Version them there (v1, v2...) when they change.

## 6. Agent team

Three tiers. I talk only to the Project Supervisor. Each agent reads only its one-page role card in `agents/`, `agents/COMMON.md` and the series template. Mechanical work is done by scripts in `agents/scripts/`, not by agents.

### Org chart
```
                         ME (editor in chief)
                                 │
              PROJECT SUPERVISOR (Opus 5.5, high effort)
                 │                                   │
  GM A: Content and Accuracy               GM B: Visual and Build
  (Sonnet 5.5, medium)                     (Sonnet 5.5, medium; multi-post runs only)
  ├─ A1 Planner-Writer                     ├─ B1 Visual and Packaging
  └─ A2 Compliance Reviewer                └─ on call: B2 Slide Assembler, B3 Image QA, B4 Product Builder

  workers: Haiku 5.5, medium effort
```

### Role cards
| Agent | Card | Job |
|---|---|---|
| Project Supervisor | agents/supervisor.md | Brief, plan approval, freeze review, final report to me |
| GM A | agents/gm-a.md | Runs Team A in order; **is the accuracy check** |
| GM B | agents/gm-b.md | Multi-post runs only: runs scripts, briefs B1, final sync check |
| A1 Planner-Writer | agents/a1-planner-writer.md | Plan, then slides.md in the canonical format (merged planner and writer) |
| A2 Compliance Reviewer | agents/a2-compliance.md | Paraphrase closeness and AHPRA only (scripts do the counting) |
| B1 Visual and Packaging | agents/b1-visual-packaging.md | Polishes the script output: layouts, alt text, caption, Pinterest (merged designer and packager) |
| B2 Slide Assembler | agents/b2-slide-assembler.md | On call: Mode A render after 2 Image QA fails |
| B3 Image QA | agents/b3-image-qa.md | On call: checks my ChatGPT images word for word |
| B4 Product Builder | agents/b4-product-builder.md | On call: QBank, Anki, notes |

### Scripts (no tokens, no waiting)
| Script | Replaces |
|---|---|
| check_text.py | Word counts, dash checks and US spelling checks that agents used to do by hand |
| build_package.py | Hand-written prompts, alt-text scaffolds, caption skeleton and checklist (from the frozen slides.md and agents/brand/style-block.md) |
| check_sync.py | Multiple consistency loops: one final check that prompts and alt text match the slides word for word |

### Rules
1. **Sequential within a post, parallel across posts.** No reviewer works on a draft that can still change.
2. **Freeze before build.** The Supervisor's last text review happens before Team B starts. After the freeze, no edits.
3. **Claims need a source:** workers may only write claims with a source page or a confirmed guideline reference.
4. **Loops:** max 2 for accuracy, max 1 compliance re-check, then escalate.
5. **Idle agents are not briefed.** On-call agents run only when needed.
6. **GM B only on multi-post runs.** On a single post, the Supervisor briefs B1 directly (the one exception to "workers never talk to the Supervisor").
7. **Batch by default:** one upload produces 4 posts to spread the start-up cost.
8. **Light logging:** one line per hand-off in `log/FLOW.md`.

### Accuracy
There is no separate Fact Checker. GM A (Sonnet) verifies every specific against the source page or a confirmed open guideline, and I do the final check (doses and thresholds always by me).

### Expected cost
Compared with v0.5 (about 13 minutes and close to a million tokens for one post): roughly half the tokens and 8 to 10 minutes per post, falling further per post when 4 run together.

### What stays with me
- Talking to the Project Supervisor (the only agent I deal with)
- Checkpoint 1: approve each post plan
- Checkpoint 2: final read-through against the guideline (doses and thresholds always checked manually)
- Generating ChatGPT images and saving them
- Final fact check of every post and product
- Final design review (Canva only for manual fixes)
- Scheduling and posting
- Anything involving money, accounts or legal and compliance decisions

## 10. Compliance and rules

- **AHPRA:** Code of conduct and social media guidance apply even with an anonymous account. Educational content only.
- **No patient content:** No cases, images or identifiable details from work, even de-identified.
- **No individual advice:** Don't answer personal medical questions in comments or DMs.
- **Copyright:** All content original. Don't copy from eTG, UpToDate, AMH, textbooks, other creators or paid QBanks.
- **Disclosure:** Clearly label affiliate links and any paid partnerships (Australian Consumer Law).
- **AI content:** Disclose AI-generated imagery where Meta requires it.
- **No supplement or health product sponsorships.**
- **WA Health:** Declare outside business activity if required.
- **Tax:** ABN, record all income and expenses, report to ATO. GST only applies above A$75k turnover.
