No single test separates youth diabetes types

No single test separates youth T1DM from T2DM, so weigh weight, age, antibodies, insulin resistance and family history together.
When the pattern is young, insulin sensitive and without islet autoimmunity, consider monogenic diabetes and confirm by gene sequencing.
Classify with the full picture, not a single feature.
