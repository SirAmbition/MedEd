Symptoms plus random 11.1 mmol/L: diagnosed today

Classic symptoms plus a random glucose of 11.1 mmol/L or more diagnose diabetes, so an OGTT is seldom needed.
Draw islet antibodies before insulin starts, and check C-peptide once the child has recovered.
Ask directly about night wetting and thirst, because the history is often missed.
