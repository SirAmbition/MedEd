T1DM risk by relative: fathers outrank mothers

Family history raises risk, and the size of the rise depends on who is affected.
A father with T1DM carries a higher risk to a child than a mother does, and a monozygotic twin carries the highest.
Use the table as a guide, and remember these are lifetime risks unless a time frame is stated.
