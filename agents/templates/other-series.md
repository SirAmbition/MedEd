# Series templates: Quick Ref, Friday Case, Side by Side

Use the same rubric lines as templates/approach.md (Level, Specificity, Sourcing, Originality; replace "Sequence" with the line below).

## Quick Ref (Wednesday, 1 image)
One table or grouped list that a registrar would screenshot: criteria, score components, thresholds or drug choices. Max ~60 words on the image. Rubric line "Usefulness": would someone save this for the ward?

## Friday Case (Friday, 5 slides)
1 vignette with real data (obs, bloods, ECG or imaging findings) and "Fictional case" label; 2 the question; 3 five plausible options A to E; 4 answer with the discriminating clue; 5 why each distractor is wrong plus the teaching point. RACP/AMC standard. Rubric line "Discrimination": the answer hinges on one specific clue, and distractors are defensible.

## Side by Side (Sunday, 5 to 7 slides)
Two or three things that are commonly confused, compared on the features that actually discriminate (not the ones they share). Rubric line "Discrimination": each row separates the conditions.
