# Series template: Approach (Monday, 10 slides)

An Approach post is a WORK-UP SEQUENCE a registrar would follow, not a list of facts. Each slide is one decision in that sequence.

## Slide structure
1. Hook: a counter-intuitive, specific claim about the topic (a number or a trap), max 8 words.
2. Trigger: when to think of it, framed as who is easy to miss (atypical or silent presentations, high-risk groups).
3. Risk stratify: the factors or score that change urgency or management (name the score or criteria).
4. Immediate decision: who needs what now (e.g. scope vs non-invasive test; urgent vs routine), with the threshold or criteria.
5. Test 1: the key investigation and its specific limitation or trap.
6. Test 2: second investigation or the "how to do it properly" detail (timing, washout, technique).
7. Interpreting results: what changes management; the false negative or false reassurance.
8. Cause and next step: finding and acting on the underlying cause.
9. Registrar pearl: the one thing most interns get wrong (must be new to a PGY1).
10. Summary: the whole work-up as a one-line sequence, plus the save CTA.

## Rules
- Every slide carries a specific: a number, threshold, score, criterion, decision rule, pitfall or mechanism.
- No slide is a definition or a list a PGY1 already knows.
- Headline max 8 words, body max 35 words.

## Rubric (score 1 to 5; 4+ on every line to pass)
| Line | 5 looks like |
|---|---|
| Level | Nothing a PGY1 already knows; at least one pearl most registrars would value |
| Sequence | Reads as a work-up a registrar would actually follow, slide by slide |
| Specificity | Every slide has a concrete number, rule, score or pitfall |
| Sourcing | Every specific traceable to a source page or a confirmable open guideline |
| Originality | Structure and wording our own, not the source's |
